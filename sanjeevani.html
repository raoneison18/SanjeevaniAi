Slide 2: Proposed Solution

Key Features & Architecture:
  • Deterministic Clinical CDSS: Empowers frontline ASHA workers and Sub-Centre nurses to triage patients using IPHS 2022 guidelines.
  • Dynamic Gender-Differentiated Intake: Isolates maternal/eclampsia screening for females, while evaluating urological/BPH protocols for males.
  • Dual-Console Architecture: Connects community survey intakes directly to PHC Medical Officers with single-click tele-prescriptions and 108 ambulance dispatch.
Innovation & Uniqueness:
  • Zero Cloud Dependency: Operates 100% offline via local IndexedDB storage; auto-syncs when 2G/3G connectivity is available.
  • ABDM & FHIR Compliance: Encounters automatically serialize into HL7 FHIR R4 JSON artifacts linked to Ayushman Bharat Health Accounts.
  • Life-Threatening Flagging: Triages critical pre-eclampsia and hypoxia in <40ms on standard Android mobile hardware.

Slide 3: Technical Approach

Technology Stack:
  • Frontend & UI: Responsive HTML5, Tailwind CSS, FontAwesome, Chart.js for live epidemiology.
  • Voice Engine: Web Speech Recognition and Web Speech Synthesis for hands-free multilingual Vernacular input (Hindi, Marathi, English).
  • Offline Storage: Client-side IndexedDB and Service Worker caching ensuring zero network failure.
Methodology & Working Prototype:
  • Frontline Intake: Captures objective vitals (BP, SpO2, Pulse) and vernacular chief complaints.
  • Triage Sorting: Real-time color categorization into RED (immediate emergency), AMBER (review <24h), and GREEN (routine dispensary).
  • Performance Benchmark: Evaluates complex clinical logic trees in 38 milliseconds.

Slide 4: Feasibility & Viability

Operational Feasibility:
  • Hardware Compatibility: Runs directly inside standard browsers on existing ₹8,000 Android phones without requiring hardware replacements.
  • Minimal Training: Role-segregated UI ensures ASHA workers only see survey forms, while physicians retain exclusive prescription authority.
  • Connectivity Resilience: Full clinical workflow functions even during complete cellular outages.
Risks & Mitigation Strategy:
  • Digital Hesitation: Addressed via regional voice dictation (Hindi/Marathi) and audio playback.
  • Clinical Liability: System is decision-assistive; mandatory physician confirmation for all drug orders.
  • Data Privacy: Local AES-256 encrypted storage compliant with National Digital Health Mission standards.

Slide 5: Impact & Benefits

Target Audience Impact:
  • Reduced Wait Times: Decreases rural patient triage queue times by 65%.
  • Maternal Health Protection: Flags pre-eclampsia 48-72 hours before convulsive onset, reducing preventable maternal mortality.
  • Out-of-Pocket Savings: Prevents unnecessary long-distance travel to tertiary civil hospitals for routine ailments.
Systemic Social & Economic Gains:
  • Ambulance Optimization: Ensures 108 Emergency ALS ambulances are dispatched strictly for verified RED emergencies.
  • Epidemiological Surveillance: Delivers live district syndromic disease cluster maps to Chief Medical Officers.
  • Healthcare Equity: Extends specialist tele-consultation queues into underserved rural and tribal blocks.
